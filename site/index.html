The area

Where the Minho meets the Atlantic

Caminha sits at the northern edge of Portugal, between the river, the ocean and the hills, with Spain on the other bank.

Foz do Minho and Camarido

Beach · all year

The northernmost sea beach in Portugal, reached through the Camarido pine forest on a 1 km boardwalk, facing the Ínsua fort.

Moledo

Beach · summer

A Blue Flag beach of sand and rock, popular for surf, bodyboard and windsurf.

Ecovia do Minho

Walking and cycling · spring and autumn

A nearly flat path along the river from Caminha to Vila Nova de Cerveira and on to Valença.

Serra d'Arga

Trails · all year

Signposted mountain trails, granite villages and natural pools for summer swims.

Monte de Santa Tecla

Spain · about 30 min by car

An ancient hillfort with views over the river mouth, on the Spanish bank. Drive across the bridge at Vila Nova de Cerveira.

Viana do Castelo and Valença

Day trips · about 25 min

Santa Luzia and the historic centre of Viana to the south, the walled fortress of Valença to the north.

Winter

Lamprey season in local restaurants from January, Christmas lights in the town square and quiet beaches.

Spring

Green hills, cycling along the river and the first lunches outside.

Summer

Beaches, the pool and music festivals: Vilar de Mouros is in the municipality, Paredes de Coura is close by.

Autumn

Mountain trails, mild days by the sea and a calm base for remote work.
